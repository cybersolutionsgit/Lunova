Jeans shopper

“model an jeans” by Grauwolf, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Adapted for Lunova: normalized scale and floor origin, reduced topology, resized/compressed original textures, and added a lower-body skeleton and an in-place walking animation. Both arms were reposed onto the trolley handle; the original contact geometry and small denim area were repaired. Creator credit is retained. Desktop and Lightweight variants use the same source model.

Other store fixtures, trolleys, basket and cashier are procedural project geometry.
